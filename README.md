# Specularis Capital

**See Clearly. Think Deeply. Move Quietly.**

Specularis Capital is a personal research and operating system focused on financial intelligence, AI workflows, learning systems, and life optimization.

## Core System

- **Financial Capital** — Market intelligence, trading systems, options, futures, and risk frameworks.
- **Intelligence Capital** — AI tools, agents, automation workflows, and productivity systems.
- **Learning Capital** — English learning, skill acquisition, and cognitive systems.
- **Physical Capital** — Fitness, health, energy management, and lifestyle optimization.
- **System Capital** — Personal knowledge infrastructure and long-term operating systems.
